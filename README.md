Turn any photo into striking GTA-style poster art: a 9-panel cover grid, a hero duo, or a single hero — graded sunset, neon night, or pastel vice, with bold staggered caption lettering — and export a print-ready PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350, or 1920×1080. Fully offline: your photos never leave your device.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Drop in a photo (or load a bundled sample), click a panel to select it, drag to reposition its photo, pick a layout and grade, type your caption, and hit **Export PNG**.
